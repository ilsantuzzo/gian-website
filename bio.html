Disciplina dell'attenzione applicata alla materia.

My practice begins with attention: a sustained physical encounter with matter.

Through contact, repetition and resistance, I explore the shifting distance between perception and form, presence and absence, control and surrender.

The body becomes both instrument and measure, while matter remains an active participant in the process.

Each work emerges from this encounter, somewhere between intention and discovery.
